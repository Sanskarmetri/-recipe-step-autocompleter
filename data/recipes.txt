Chop the onions into small pieces.
Heat oil in a pan.
Add chopped onions and fry until golden brown.
Add ginger garlic paste and cook for two minutes.
Add tomatoes and stir well.
Add salt and turmeric powder.
Cook until the mixture becomes soft.
Garnish with fresh coriander leaves.
Serve hot.

Wash the rice thoroughly with water.
Soak rice for thirty minutes.
Boil water in a large pot.
Add rice to boiling water.
Cook until the rice becomes soft.
Drain the excess water.
Serve with curry.

Heat butter in a saucepan.
Add chopped garlic and saute until fragrant.
Add chopped mushrooms.
Cook for five minutes.
Add fresh cream and stir well.
Season with salt and black pepper.
Simmer until the sauce thickens.
Serve hot with pasta.
